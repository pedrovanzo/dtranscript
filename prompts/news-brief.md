Produce a newsroom research brief (internal use, not for publication as-is) with exactly these sections:

## Headline suggestions
3 short, factual headline options. No clickbait.

## Lead
One paragraph answering who, what, when, where and why, using only what the transcript states.

## Facts stated in the video
Bullets of concrete statements, each with its [mm:ss] timestamp and who said it (if identifiable).

## Quotes
Verbatim quotes usable as sources, each as `> "quote" — speaker (if identifiable), [mm:ss]`.

## Claims to verify
Every claim that requires independent checking before publication, with timestamp and why it needs checking.

## Sensitive topics
Flag if the content touches elections, public security, health, accusations, reputation or individual rights. Write "None identified" otherwise.

## Suggested tags
5-8 lowercase tags.
